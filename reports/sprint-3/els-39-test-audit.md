## Three corrections and counterexamples

W1 concerns complete historical results, not just whether filtering executes. The corrected student-one result must contain exactly the known active and returned loans in ascending order; student two must contain exactly its own due-today loan. Both student-one DTOs are checked with the existing exact six-field/value helper. The original test remains useful for combined-filter behavior; its incomplete student-only oracle is the corrected weakness.

W2 concerns the timezone of a deterministic business date. The old fixed instant 2030-06-15T09:00:00Z hides UTC substitution. The new fixed instant 2030-06-14T21:30:00Z is already the next business day in Asia/Beirut. Existing due-yesterday/today/returned fixtures and assertions now discriminate the two dates. This is not an execution-day test or cosmetic timestamp change.

W3 concerns which reference owns the active-loan limit. The old direct SQL test repeats both keys. Two new SQL cases vary them independently: one student may hold two distinct equipment items, while a second student may not take the first student's occupied item. The latter asserts structured SQLSTATE 23505 and ux_loan_active_equipment, then verifies the failed insert left original rows unchanged. These cases test the database boundary directly rather than relying on service locking to mask a wrong index. The external ELS-36 workflow exercises several items per student, but is not the direct SQL/index gate in normal Maven verification.
